# Data sources and attribution

- Terrain and aerial imagery: © swisstopo. See https://www.swisstopo.admin.ch/en/terms-and-conditions and the manifests under public/terrain. The mosaics are historical, not live snow-condition imagery. Retain swisstopo attribution when displaying or redistributing these derived assets.
- Routes, hydrology and Swiss–Italian reference border: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . Individual GeoJSON files retain source URLs and snapshots. The border is mapped reference geometry, not a newly surveyed legal boundary.
- Weather-model data: Open-Meteo https://open-meteo.com/en/docs and https://open-meteo.com/en/terms . Retrieved at runtime; units, elevation and timestamps are shown.
- Camera frames and viewer: © Zermatt Bergbahnen / Roundshot. Retrieved at runtime from official endpoints. No ownership or open-source license to the camera photographs is claimed. The hosted recorded-example photographs are excluded from this public source export.
- Zmuttgletscher historical area: Mölg et al. (2019), The Cryosphere, §4.2, https://tc.copernicus.org/articles/13/1889/2019/ . About 26% less area is calculated from 21.24 km² (1859) and 15.74 km² (2016); it is not ice-volume loss or current snow cover.
- Swiss glacier volume: GLAMOS annual report 2025, page 10, https://doi.glamos.ch/pubs/annualrep/annualrep_2025.pdf . National 2015–2025 estimate; not a local piste measurement.
- Protect Our Winters Switzerland: https://www.protectourwinters.ch/de/unsere-arbeit/ and https://www.protectourwinters.ch/de/handeln/ . Educational links only; no affiliation or endorsement.
